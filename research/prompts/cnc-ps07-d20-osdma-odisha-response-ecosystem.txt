# CNC PS-07 WAVE 3 — OSDMA + ODISHA DISASTER RESPONSE ECOSYSTEM

OFFICIAL PROBLEM STATEMENT (verbatim, Craft N Code 2026):
Title: Cyclone & Flood-Resilient Smart Agriculture Advisory System
Body: Design an AI/IoT-based early-warning and advisory platform that integrates IMD alerts with hyperlocal farm data to recommend pre-disaster actions and post-disaster recovery steps for farmers in Odisha. It must work via SMS/IVR for low-literacy users.
Objectives:
1. Minimize crop loss during extreme weather events.
2. Ensure accessibility for low-literacy and non-smartphone users.
3. Provide actionable, time-sensitive agricultural advice.
Features:
1. Integration with IMD alerts and local farm data profiles.
2. Automated advisory generation for pre and post-disaster.
3. SMS and IVR (Voice) delivery system.

## Core line
Statement-faithful research for the KrishiSetu edge-AI advisory system. This run
maps what exists, what is real, and what a prototype can use today.

Research the institutional response ecosystem KrishiSetu must plug into:
- OSDMA (Odisha State Disaster Management Authority): structure, cyclone preparedness program, early warning dissemination, dashboards and open data, their app (any), how warnings reach villages (sirens, volunteers, WhatsApp).
- ODRAF (Odisha Disaster Rapid Action Force) + NDRF deployment patterns.
- Cyclone shelters: how many, where (coastal Odisha), capacity, who manages, the shelter + livestock shelter program.
- The Disaster Management Act 2005 roles, district collectors, block-level machinery, the last-mile warning chain (who tells the farmer).
- KALIA + agriculture dept disaster response: crop loss assessment process after cyclones, the 7-day assessment (Dana: 5,428 acres), compensation flow.
- Fani/Yaas/Dana after-action reports: what worked, what failed in warning dissemination, response timelines, lessons.
- The white space: what the state cannot do (hyperlocal, per-plot, personalized, stage-aware) that our system does.
- Integration points: how a startup/product plugs into OSDMA/KVK/block machinery (MoUs, pilot programs, the extension worker channel).
- Published evaluations of Odisha's early warning system (the "gold standard" claims, what studies say).
