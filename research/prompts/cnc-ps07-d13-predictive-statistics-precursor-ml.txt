# CNC PS-07 EDGE-AI WAVE — PREDICTIVE STATISTICS + ML FOR PRECURSOR DETECTION

OFFICIAL PROBLEM STATEMENT (verbatim, Craft N Code 2026):
Title: Cyclone & Flood-Resilient Smart Agriculture Advisory System
Body: Design an AI/IoT-based early-warning and advisory platform that integrates IMD alerts with hyperlocal farm data to recommend pre-disaster actions and post-disaster recovery steps for farmers in Odisha. It must work via SMS/IVR for low-literacy users.
Objectives:
1. Minimize crop loss during extreme weather events.
2. Ensure accessibility for low-literacy and non-smartphone users.
3. Provide actionable, time-sensitive agricultural advice.
Features:
1. Integration with IMD alerts and local farm data profiles.
2. Automated advisory generation for pre and post-disaster.
3. SMS and IVR (Voice) delivery system.

## Core line
Statement-faithful research for the edge-AI mesh architecture: cheap sensor nodes,
farmer phone as offline AI hub (small quantized LLM), continuous learning loop
(server fine-tunes, phone adapts), and the predictive statistics underneath. This
run maps what exists, what is real vs marketing, and what a prototype can demo.

Research the mathematical methods for turning cheap sensor streams into early warnings:
- Bayesian Online Changepoint Detection (Adams-MacKay 2007): applications to pressure/wind series, implementation cost, published uses in meteorology.
- Cyclone precursor science: published pressure-drop signatures before Bay of Bengal cyclones (hPa/hour), wind pattern shifts, humidity saturation, cloud/IR satellite precursors; how early can local sensors detect vs satellite?
- Flood precursor science: rainfall intensity-duration thresholds for flash floods, river gauge rise rates (CWC data), soil saturation indices, antecedent precipitation index; what local sensors can see.
- Sensor fusion: Kalman filters for noisy cheap sensors, particle filters, published low-cost weather station fusion results.
- Anomaly detection: isolation forest, EWMA adaptive thresholds, Gaussian process novelty; online (streaming) versions.
- Extreme Value Theory: GEV/GPD return periods for flood depth and wind speed at Indian stations; how a plot-level "floods at depth X every N years" prior is built.
- Spatiotemporal models: kriging/GP interpolation from sparse village sensors, published Indian applications.
- Any published work combining on-device ML + cheap sensors for cyclone/flood warning in India or the global south.
