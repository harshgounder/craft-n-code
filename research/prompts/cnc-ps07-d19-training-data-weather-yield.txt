# CNC PS-07 WAVE 3 — TRAINING DATA: HISTORICAL WEATHER + YIELD + CROP STATISTICS

OFFICIAL PROBLEM STATEMENT (verbatim, Craft N Code 2026):
Title: Cyclone & Flood-Resilient Smart Agriculture Advisory System
Body: Design an AI/IoT-based early-warning and advisory platform that integrates IMD alerts with hyperlocal farm data to recommend pre-disaster actions and post-disaster recovery steps for farmers in Odisha. It must work via SMS/IVR for low-literacy users.
Objectives:
1. Minimize crop loss during extreme weather events.
2. Ensure accessibility for low-literacy and non-smartphone users.
3. Provide actionable, time-sensitive agricultural advice.
Features:
1. Integration with IMD alerts and local farm data profiles.
2. Automated advisory generation for pre and post-disaster.
3. SMS and IVR (Voice) delivery system.

## Core line
Statement-faithful research for the KrishiSetu edge-AI advisory system. This run
maps what exists, what is real, and what a prototype can use today.

Research what historical datasets exist to TRAIN the predictive models (and what is downloadable):
- IMD gridded rainfall/temperature data (0.25/1 degree grids, daily, 1901-present): access procedure (IMD Pune data portal, fees?), the IMD Open Data license.
- IMD cyclone best-track database (1961-present, tracks, intensity, landfall): format, access.
- CWC river gauge daily data: India-WRIS, years of record.
- Crop yield statistics: district-level yields for Odisha crops (DES, Ministry of Agriculture, ICAR-DACNET), years, format.
- Cost of cultivation data (CACP): input costs per crop per state.
- ICRISAT district-level data for India (village dynamics): what it covers, access.
- PMFBY claims data: district/crop claim rates (public reports?), loss assessment reports.
- Soil Health Card data: any aggregated/APIs?
- Satellite: Sentinel-1/2 free archives (Copernicus), NDVI products, flood extent maps (NASA GPM, GDACS), any India-specific flood layers (Bhuvan).
- NSSO/ASUSE surveys for farmer economics.
- What is actually downloadable today by a student team vs what needs purchase/MOU.
