# CNC PS-07 WAVE 3 — WHO PAYS: BUYER, WILLINGNESS-TO-PAY, SUBSIDY RAILS

OFFICIAL PROBLEM STATEMENT (verbatim, Craft N Code 2026):
Title: Cyclone & Flood-Resilient Smart Agriculture Advisory System
Body: Design an AI/IoT-based early-warning and advisory platform that integrates IMD alerts with hyperlocal farm data to recommend pre-disaster actions and post-disaster recovery steps for farmers in Odisha. It must work via SMS/IVR for low-literacy users.
Objectives:
1. Minimize crop loss during extreme weather events.
2. Ensure accessibility for low-literacy and non-smartphone users.
3. Provide actionable, time-sensitive agricultural advice.
Features:
1. Integration with IMD alerts and local farm data profiles.
2. Automated advisory generation for pre and post-disaster.
3. SMS and IVR (Voice) delivery system.

## Core line
Statement-faithful research for the KrishiSetu edge-AI advisory system. This run
maps what exists, what is real, and what a prototype can use today.

Research the economics and buyers for a farm advisory + sensor + edge-AI product in Odisha:
- Willingness to pay: published studies on Indian farmers paying for advisory services (any numbers), what DeHaat/Fasal charge farmers or FPOs, subscription economics in agritech India.
- Institutional buyers: who pays for farmer outreach today (state agriculture dept, KVKs, OSDMA, NABARD, FPOs, input companies, insurers, PMFBY), budget lines that could fund this (KALIA, RKVY, Digital Agri Mission Rs 2,817 cr, sub-missions on agri extension).
- KALIA scheme: Odisha's own farmer support scheme, what it funds, whether advisory fits.
- PMFBY angle: insurers pay for loss assessment and advisory? any precedent of insurer-funded advisory (crop insurance + advisory bundles)?
- Sensor/IoT procurement precedent: has any Indian state bought agri IoT at scale (e.g., Gujarat IoT project, any smart-farming pilots), tender sizes.
- Competitor pricing: Fasal subscription, DeHaat advisory, Gramophone, weather subscription services (Skymet), what farmers actually pay.
- The B2B2C path: FPOs and cooperatives as distribution, subsidy co-pay models.
- Any published willingness-to-pay or adoption economics for agritech in Odisha specifically.
