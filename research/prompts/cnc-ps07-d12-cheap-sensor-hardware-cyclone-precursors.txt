# CNC PS-07 EDGE-AI WAVE — CHEAP SENSOR HARDWARE: CYCLONE/FLOOD PRECURSORS UNDER RS 3,000

OFFICIAL PROBLEM STATEMENT (verbatim, Craft N Code 2026):
Title: Cyclone & Flood-Resilient Smart Agriculture Advisory System
Body: Design an AI/IoT-based early-warning and advisory platform that integrates IMD alerts with hyperlocal farm data to recommend pre-disaster actions and post-disaster recovery steps for farmers in Odisha. It must work via SMS/IVR for low-literacy users.
Objectives:
1. Minimize crop loss during extreme weather events.
2. Ensure accessibility for low-literacy and non-smartphone users.
3. Provide actionable, time-sensitive agricultural advice.
Features:
1. Integration with IMD alerts and local farm data profiles.
2. Automated advisory generation for pre and post-disaster.
3. SMS and IVR (Voice) delivery system.

## Core line
Statement-faithful research for the edge-AI mesh architecture: cheap sensor nodes,
farmer phone as offline AI hub (small quantized LLM), continuous learning loop
(server fine-tunes, phone adapts), and the predictive statistics underneath. This
run maps what exists, what is real vs marketing, and what a prototype can demo.

Research the cheapest sensor stack that can detect cyclone/flood precursors:
- Barometric pressure (BMP280/BME280): accuracy, drift, cost, why pressure drop rate is the best cheap cyclone precursor, published precursor signatures (hPa drop rates before BoB cyclones, typical lead times).
- Temperature + humidity (BME280/DHT22/SHT31): accuracy vs cost.
- Wind: cheap anemometer options (DIY cup anemometer, ultrasonic cheap units), wind gust detection, thresholds.
- Rain: tipping bucket DIY vs commercial, calibration.
- Soil moisture (capacitive probes), water level (float, ultrasonic), UV (VEML6075/ML8511), light (BH1750).
- Node controller: ESP32 vs ESP8266 vs Raspberry Pi Zero 2W vs Arduino: price, ADC quality, Bluetooth for phone pairing, deep sleep power, LoRa option for village mesh (RAK/SX1276), GSM module cost.
- Solar + battery sizing for a rural node, enclosure/weatherproofing DIY.
- Total node bill of materials in Indian rupees from actual Indian vendors (Robu, Mouser India, Amazon India, local shops).
- Any published farm-sensor deployments in Odisha/India with real cost and failure data.
