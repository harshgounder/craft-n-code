# CNC PS-07 WAVE 3 — POWER + CONNECTIVITY REALITY IN RURAL ODISHA

OFFICIAL PROBLEM STATEMENT (verbatim, Craft N Code 2026):
Title: Cyclone & Flood-Resilient Smart Agriculture Advisory System
Body: Design an AI/IoT-based early-warning and advisory platform that integrates IMD alerts with hyperlocal farm data to recommend pre-disaster actions and post-disaster recovery steps for farmers in Odisha. It must work via SMS/IVR for low-literacy users.
Objectives:
1. Minimize crop loss during extreme weather events.
2. Ensure accessibility for low-literacy and non-smartphone users.
3. Provide actionable, time-sensitive agricultural advice.
Features:
1. Integration with IMD alerts and local farm data profiles.
2. Automated advisory generation for pre and post-disaster.
3. SMS and IVR (Voice) delivery system.

## Core line
Statement-faithful research for the KrishiSetu edge-AI advisory system. This run
maps what exists, what is real, and what a prototype can use today.

Research the infrastructure reality that shapes the offline-first design:
- Mobile network coverage in coastal Odisha districts: 4G/3G/2G availability by district (TRAI reports, Ookla, OpenSignal India data), which areas have poor coverage.
- What happens to telecom towers during cyclones: power loss, fiber cuts, battery backup duration, tower failure stats from Fani/Amphan/Yaas/Dana, how long connectivity drops.
- Electricity reliability: hours of power per day in rural Odisha (state DISCOM data, surveys), how this affects sensor nodes and phone charging.
- Solar viability: insolation in Odisha (MNRE data), cost of small solar kits, battery sizing for a sensor node.
- The offline window math: during a cyclone, how long is a village cut off (communication + power + roads)? Studies and reports.
- Smartphone penetration in rural Odisha: NFHS/ASER/TRAI numbers, basic phone share, data pack affordability.
- What survived vs failed in past cyclones: community radio, ham radio (300 operators in Kerala floods), VSAT, satellite phones, cell broadcast (does India use it?).
- Implications: what the design must assume (offline hours, power cycles, bandwidth).
