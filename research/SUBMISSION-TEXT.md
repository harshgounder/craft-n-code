## ABSTRACT (one paragraph)

KrishiSetu joins a consented farm profile (crop, variety, stage, plot
elevation, soil, tenancy, labor) to authoritative IMD and CWC signals
and compiles crop-stage-specific pre and post disaster actions, each
with a deadline, a source, a cost of waiting, and a doability line
(labor hours, cost, credit, tenancy). Advisory actions are ranked by
what the farmer can actually do, and delivered through a degraded-mode
ladder: app, SMS, IVR, USSD, community radio, village volunteer, so a
feature phone or a dead tower never blocks the message. The system
tracks each incident through 11 states, validates itself against
replayed real events (Fani 108,220 ha and Rs 1,304.58 crore of
assessed crop loss, Yaas surge and rainfall), and assembles a claims
packet with the PMFBY 72-hour intimation clock and the 33% state
relief threshold. Every number on screen wears one of four evidence
badges: ODISHA-MEASURED, TRANSFER-PRIOR, SCENARIO-ASSUMPTION,
UNKNOWN. The LLM renders; the audited rule engine decides.

## FEATURE BULLETS (3)

1. One warning, two farms, different advice: the same cyclone warning
   produces different actions for Asha (flowering paddy, low plot,
   weak embankment, leased land: protect seed, shelter livestock,
   no harvest, tenancy-blocked embankment) and a high-field farm
   (mature paddy, owned land: harvest now). Doability gates every
   action: labor, cost, credit, tenancy.

2. Delivery that survives the disaster: a 6-rung degraded-mode ladder
   with per-rung latency budgets and automatic escalation on
   unacknowledged messages, from app down to village volunteer, plus
   offline rendering from local state when bandwidth dies.

3. Numbers that prove themselves: replay validation against Fani and
   Yaas anchors, a 49-report evidence base across 7 waves with a searchable research
   panel, an enforced evidence chain (slide to ledger to index to
   raw), and a claims packet that rides the PMFBY 72-hour intimation
   window.

SIMULATOR, SIMULATED STREAM or ROADMAP, and every number wears one of
