Identification aligns with the club's flagship "CSC X IIITBHUB" highlight and "NOVUS'25" fest references. -> A cybersecurity track (auth, threat-detection, supply-chain, OSINT, identity) gets thematic tailwind even when the problem statement is general.
- **SIH Reward Floor = ₹1,50,000**: All SIH 2025 grand finale winners (and SIH 2024) carry INR 1,50,000 prize money per team [executive_summary[2]] [12]. -> Calibrate your Craft N Code ambition: the "national finale" prize pool is in the same band as the second-tier NHAI / ministry hacks.
- **State-Level Pathways Are Multi-Channel**: Beyond SIH, iStart Rajasthan + IIMCIP ran a MeitY TIDE 2.0 Ideation Hackathon ("Nurturing Innovation: Ideas to Impact - Empowering Rajasthan") on 19 January 2026 with 23 startups pitching to an IIMCIP + iStart panel [executive_summary[3]] [2]; Rajasthan Innovation Award jury selects via government-notified criteria. -> Rajasthan's state ecosystem rewards govt-aligned, scalable, panel-defensible pitches, not just working demos.
- **Unstop Patterns Reward Rounds**: The same Unstop infrastructure that runs Craft N Code drives Adobe University Hackathon 2026 (Round 1: 15 MCQ in 15 min + 2 coding in 45 min on 9-10 Aug; Round 2: APT interview with Pre-Placement Interviews prize). -> Speed and accuracy under time pressure matter for the first filter; APT-round style gives bonus weight to articulation.
- **Adoption, Scale, and Agentic AI Dominate 2025-26 Winners**: Google Cloud Gen AI 2025 top 100 teams, presented to a 30-member jury, winning entries emphasised youth mental-wellness conversational AI, AI-artisan marketplaces, RAG legal-document intelligence and explainable deepfake detection - "shift away from prototype demos toward agentic systems and explainable models". -> Pick a category where adoption > novelty; build for agents + explainability.
- **Failure Case Studies Are Public**: Reddit r/Btechtards user lost SIH 2024 "for no clear reason" despite being "the only team following the problem statement" (PS 1779); the high-profile Dev.to "From Rejection to Recognition" piece shows that failed hackathons can be rebranded as bigger wins with persistence. -> Expect ambiguity; document your PS adherence.
- **Demo-Day Reality Check**: "Most student hackathon backends are mocked, hard-coded, or duct-taped together - and somehow, judges still clap". Many hackathon projects die after demo day because they were "built for judging, not for continuation". -> Both win and lose; plan a real-data fallback to defend when judges probe live.
- **Five-Judge Devpost Consensus Wins**: Devpost's "Advice from 5 seasoned judges" piece shows judges reward creativity + execution + functional MVP in roughly equal weight. -> Hit all three dimensions; do not over-index on novelty alone.
- **Re-Use Pattern Across Rajasthan**: Nexora'26 - a *separate* state-level online hackathon organised by the Cyber Space Club at MUJ, "exclusively open to students from Rajasthan institutions" - runs on exactly the same CSC MUJ playbook: only-Rajasthan eligibility, online first round, top teams advance. -> The MUJ-CSC pipeline (Nexora -> Craft N Code -> IIIT National Finale) is a deliberate ladder; align Craft N Code submissions with the same template.

**Take-aways** (with the four-step framework):

- **Observation**: Judging is multi-criterion and *inconsistent*; large panels tilt toward theme-fit ("Speak to Agents" for Adobe; "agentic systems and explainable models" for Google Gen AI).
- **Mechanism**: Judges reward *demo polish* easily but cannot always verify *backend correctness* in 24 hours. They also weight the *story arc* (problem-fidelity + impact projection).
- **Implication**: A team that builds agentic AI or AI-for-security within the CSC MUJ cybersecurity identity and ties its pitch to the same language judges use ("agentic", "explainable", "adoption") clears the bar faster.
- **Recommendation**: (a) one slide on PS-fidelity line-by-line; (b) one live-data flow, not a mock; (c) one "wow" angle (RAG + explainability + auth trail) so a senior jury member can cite it; (d) one sentence mapping the project to a national-scale deployment.

**Take-aways**:

- **Failure mode #1 - PS-fidelity paradox**: The SIH 2024 Reddit post-mortem demonstrates the paradox where the team **most loyal to the brief lost**. That is a *systemic signal*: in large panels, the theme and audience resonance often trump PS-precision.
- **Failure mode #2 - Demo vs backend gap**: Judges reward visible polish; mocked/hard-coded backends "still get claps". Teams that *also* surface a real-data backbone when probed tend to win consistently.
- **Failure mode #3 - Scope creep**: Hackathon Radar notes most projects die because they were *built for judging* (high polish, low moat) rather than for continuation. To avoid losing to scope creep, pad 1-2 "wow" layers, not 5 stretches.
- **Recovery is real**: The "From Rejection to Recognition" piece shows a failed hackathon became a startup-grade win; treat losses as compounding signal, not verdict.

## 9_comparative_synthesis_what_each_tier_demands

| Dimension | State Hackathon (Craft N Code) | National Unstop-Sponsored Hackathon (Adobe, HackX) | Mega-Hackathon (SIH, Google Gen AI) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Demo length | 5-7 min live + Q&A 3-5 min | 3-5 min PPT + 5-7 min demo | 36-hour build, 8-10 min final demo + jury Q&A |
| Prize pool | ₹50,000 | ₹5,00,000 / MacBook + internship | ₹1,50,000 per team / ₹1 crore grant (Samsung) |
| Adjudicators | 2-5 faculty + alumni organisers | 5-10 industry mentors | 30+ industry-leader jury (Google Gen AI) |
| Round 1 selection | Abstract / idea-pitch | Quiz + coding (Adobe) | Multi-round ministry filtration |
| What wins | Security + agentic + IIITB-tied novelty + PS fidelity | Theme-fit, polished demo, scalability narrative | Adoption + agentic + explainability |
| Risk of loss despite PS-fidelity | Medium; documented case | Low (proper rubric) | Medium (adoption signal can override PS) |
| Path forward | Top 2 -> IIIT-Bangalore National Finale | Pre-Placement Interviews; national ranking | Grand finale winners -> MoE recognition + ₹1.5 L |

**Three strategic tensions exposed by these data points**:

- **Theme-fixity vs. agentic-novelty paradox**: Headline patterns in 2025-26 say "agentic + explainable" wins at Google; CSC MUJ's identity says "cybersecurity" wins - and IIITB's national finale gate rewards consistency. Choose whether to lead with cyber-security (CSC MUJ fit) *+ agentic* (Google fit) *or* novelty-first.
- **PS-fidelity vs. adoption paradox**: SIH 2024's Reddit failure case shows pure PS-fidelity did not save a team; adoption and judge-resonance beat PS-literalism at the higher tiers.
- **Polished demo vs. real data**: The Medium fake-backend piece, the Hackathon Radar "die after demo day" piece, and the Dev.to "From Rejection to Recognition" piece form a triangle: surface polish wins hackathons, real data + scope discipline wins *careers*.

**Recommendation for Craft N Code 2026 Teams (one-liner)**: Build a cybersecurity-flavored agentic system (RAG + threat-intel + explainable detection) with a real-data live demo, anchor the story in IIITB-style explainability, register on Unstop per CSC MUJ pipeline, and budget the 24 hours so that at least 2 hours is left for *demo rehearsal and PS-fidelity proof*.
