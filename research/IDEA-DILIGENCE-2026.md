## THE IDEA FINDER (how to pick at 21:30, 2 minutes)
1. Fingerprint company (Section 1 of TOPIC-UNIVERSE).
2. Match shape (Section 2) -> kit (Section 4).
3. Match domain (Section 3) -> this file's row for: sponsor
   alignment + judge fit + idea seed + demo gate.
4. If security words ANYWHERE -> KIT-4B overrides.
5. If the drop matches no company: PS-03 default (KIT-1) or PS-04
   open track via the matrix.
The idea is never fresh at 21:30: it is the pre-scored seed from
this file, mounted in the kit, nouns swapped. Fresh ideas after
clock start are banned (our rule).
