# RESEARCH-VISIBILITY.md — How the Depth Reaches the Judges

Date: 2026-08-16. The problem: 48 research reports, ~2.5M chars, 4,000+
cited sources, and a 10-slide deck. If the deck summarizes the research,
the depth dies. The system below guarantees: every slide claim traces to
a proof row, every proof row traces to a raw report, and the research
apparatus itself is a pitch element.

## THE THREE ARTIFACTS AND THEIR JOBS

### 1. THE DECK (the 90-second proof)
- Slide "THE RESEARCH MACHINE" (new, after the problem slide):
  - One visual: 48 parallel research runs, 6 waves, ~2.5M chars of
    evidence, 4,000+ named dated sources, every report with coverage
    tables + noise logs + A-D grades
  - One line: "single-pass AI summarizes. this is an orchestrated
    parallel research machine with verification gates. every claim in
    this deck traces to a source you can open."
  - One contrast: "we found 46 ways the world has fought floods and
    cyclones across 6 continents, graded them A-D, and kept the A/B
    ones as rules"
- Every number on every slide wears a badge: ODISHA-MEASURED /
  TRANSFER-PRIOR / SCENARIO-ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN (the d47 policy)
- Slide 8 (faculty feedback contract) becomes: "grade our decision
  quality: why this action, why now, why this channel, and whether the
  uncertainty is honest"

### 2. THE REPO (the follow-up proof)
- README top section "EVIDENCE BASE": the 48-report map, one line each,
  all in research/raw/
- EVIDENCE-INDEX.md: the master map (report -> what it proved -> which
  deck claim it feeds). Judges who ask "where is this from" get a path,
  not a shrug
- PROOF-LEDGER-2026.md rows extended: every deck number -> report file +
  section + source URL
- The repo face says: "ask us about any number on any slide. it has a
  source, a date, and a grade."

### 3. THE PROTOTYPE (the live proof)
- The trace drawer (already built in the scaffold) shows the advisory's
  full provenance: rule ID, source link, evidence grade, timestamp
- Every advisory action carries its four-badge label
- The Fani/Yaas replay panel shows the posterior vs the real anchors
  (108,220 ha / Rs 1,304.58 cr; 2-4m surge), with the uncertainty band
- The "RESEARCH" panel in the webapp: the 48-report map, searchable,
  so a judge can open a report during the demo

## THE NO-OMISSION GUARANTEE (the mechanism)
1. Every deck claim gets a PROOF-LEDGER row. A claim without a row does
   not ship (existing repo rule, enforced)
2. Every PROOF-LEDGER row gets a raw report + section + source URL.
   The evidence chain is: slide -> ledger -> report -> source
3. The EVIDENCE-INDEX covers ALL 48 reports, not just the cited ones:
   omitted reports still appear in the map, so nothing is hidden
4. The deck's appendix slide lists the full wave structure (d1-d47 +
   refires) so the scope is visible even in 10 slides
5. The four-badge policy prevents "useless summarization": a claim is
   either measured, transferred, assumed, or unknown, and it says so

## THE "HOLY SHIT" MOMENT (what the judges actually see)
- The deck slide that shows 48 runs / 6 waves / ~2.5M chars / 4,000+
  sources with the line "this took a research machine, not a chat"
- The live replay: our engine vs what actually happened in Fani, with
  the uncertainty band, in 60 seconds
- The FR13A story: an Odisha landrace became the Sub1 gene. heritage
  became technology. one sentence, lands every time
- The adaptation-gap line: "the message was never the problem. doability
  was. every advisory we send carries labor, cost, credit, and tenancy
  context, because a harvest order without labor is noise"
- The two-farm contrast: same warning, different advice, live

## WHAT NOT TO DO
- Do not put all 48 reports in the deck (death by slide)
- Do not claim "we read everything" without the index to prove it
- Do not let the research slide become a logo wall: it must show the
  MACHINE (waves, gates, grades), not a crowded map
- Do not summarize the cascade engine into "AI + IoT": the typed DAG,
  CVaR, MC convergence gates, and four badges are the depth markers
