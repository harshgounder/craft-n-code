## SYNTHESIS  -  how to win THIS panel

1. The panel is NOT "technical vs nontechnical"  -  it's two axes: research/security (Sarthak, Lingaraj, Anjana) vs operations/impact (Ayushi, Sonali, Shivani).
2. A project must satisfy BOTH: strong benchmark for Sarthak AND no data leaks for Lingaraj AND real-user safety for Anjana AND quantified value for Ayushi AND adoption narrative for Sonali.
3. The winning shape: working prototype + documented architecture + testing (Sarthak/Lingaraj) + safety/deployability framing (Anjana) + quantified workflow value (Ayushi) + quality/learning evidence (Shivani) + adoption story (Sonali).
4. For an AI project: expect Sarthak to ask about training data/overfitting/metrics, Lingaraj about authorization controls/data leakage, Anjana about abuse/privacy, Ayushi about cost reduction, Sonali about who adopts it.
5. Security angle is the differentiator: 3 of 6 judges (Anjana, Lingaraj, + Sarthak's reliability work) reward security depth. A security-flavored project (like Kavach) hits the panel's center of mass.
