## 1. THE EVIDENCE BASE (what each track's question will likely be)

### PS-01 Rewind the Legacy (100 pts)
- 2025 Challenge 5 (Inbox Navigator) = "rebuild the neglected campus Gmail experience" = the template
- 2022 D3h02/D3h03/D3h05 = hostel management, campus services, mess management = the campus-tool family
- 2024 = food safety (campus canteen) = same family
- **Most likely question**: rebuild a campus tool (mess/canteen, hostel, library, attendance, placement portal, ERP) with AI. The 2022 problems ARE the legacy tools.
- **Probability: HIGH (the family has appeared in EVERY edition)**

### PS-02 Night Ops (200 pts)
- NEW track, no direct precedent
- "sleep, safety, focus, logistics" = night-shift workers, students pulling all-nighters, delivery/logistics at night
- Sponsor angle: Accenture (workforce/enterprise ops), Google (Workspace for night teams)
- **Most likely question**: night-shift productivity/safety tool, sleep tracking, night logistics coordination, campus night safety
- **Probability: MEDIUM (new, but the club loves campus-life themes)**

### PS-03 Signal / Noise (300 pts)
- 2025 Challenge 5 (Inbox Navigator) = DIRECT predecessor (search/summarisation/ranking of emails)
- 2025 Challenge 4 (Lecture Generator) = content summarisation
- Sponsor angle: Google (search DNA), Meta (news ranking), Adobe (content)
- **Most likely question**: AI search/summariser for a specific domain (emails, news, research papers, social feeds, campus notices). The Inbox Navigator rework is the safest bet.
- **Probability: VERY HIGH (direct 2025 predecessor + Google sponsor)**

### PS-04 Open Track (400 pts)
- Anything. The escape hatch.
- 2025 winners' lane (packet hunter was effectively open-ish)
- **Most likely question**: none — self-directed. Judges reward surprise + depth.
- **Probability: N/A (no question, but the safest strategic lane)**

### PS-05 Hardware Hack (500 pts)
- 2024 food-safety solutions used Raspberry Pi Pico W + DHT-11/MQ135 sensors (adhithim09 repo)
- 2022 D3h06 = PM2.5 prediction with deployed model (sensor-adjacent)
- Sponsor angle: Apple (hardware DNA), Google (IoT)
- **Most likely question**: sensor-based campus/environment/health monitoring (air quality, canteen hygiene, energy, water), smart campus IoT
- **Probability: MEDIUM-HIGH (hardware has appeared in 2022 + 2024, and it's the highest-point lane)**
