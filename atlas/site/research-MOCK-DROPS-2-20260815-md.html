MOCK-DROPS-2-20260815.md: sponsor-DNA mock statements (wave-3 grounded)

Drill #2: statements built from wave-3 VERIFIED sponsor programs, parsed

with the machine (company -> shape -> domain -> kit -> demo gate). These

are the statements setters are MOST likely to write, given the 2026

programs we now know exist (I/O Connect India 14 Jul, Content Credentials

22 Jan, OpenEnv Apr, Accenture CRAIO).

MOCK 4: Google-flavored education mentor (ATL Saathi DNA)

Statement: "ATL Saathi-style: build a Gemini-powered mentor that helps

10,000 Atal Tinkering Labs teachers turn student projects into

measurable learning outcomes, works in Hindi and 5 other Indic

languages, and must be deployable on a school laptop with no internet."

Parse:

  • Company fingerprint: Gemini + ATL + Indic + offline -> Google (24%),

Apple (15% on-device).

  • Shape: 3 (private intelligence / on-device) + 12 (voice-first Indic).
  • Domain: education (ATL Saathi exists, AIIMS MedGemma pattern).
  • Kit: KIT-3 (offline fallback) + KIT-1 (ranking outcomes). Noun swap:

feeds = student projects, labels = learning outcomes.

  • Demo gate: Hindi voice -> project ingest -> outcome rank -> offline

flip (KILL THE NETWORK). The badge honesty moment IS the demo.

  • Ammo: ATL Saathi rolls out to 10,000 ATLs (wave-3); DeepMind 56h

curriculum; Project Vaani 109 languages.

  • Risk: on-device Hindi quality; answer = Whisper-large-v3 + Sarvam

fallback (honest about latency).

  • VERDICT: HIGH FIT, KIT-3 + KIT-1 both exist and are verified.

MOCK 5: Adobe-flavored provenance wall (Content Credentials DNA)

Statement: "Design a system that lets a brand agency verify and show

the provenance of every asset in a campaign: who captured it, what

model touched it, what was changed, and what the final consumer sees.

Judged on trust, transparency, and the failure case."

Parse:

  • Company fingerprint: provenance + assets + verification -> Adobe

(18%, Content Credentials marquee), Apple (15% on-device signing).

  • Shape: 2 (creative provenance) with shape 1 underlay (approval).
  • Domain: creative economy (D2C 11K cos, 800 funded).
  • Kit: KIT-2 (creative) + KIT-1 (approval gate). Provenance manifest =

our deterministic prompt_sha256 + audit rows, extended per asset.

  • Demo gate: one asset chain (capture -> model -> edit -> ship) with

the manifest visible per hop, then the failure case: a tampered asset

whose manifest breaks and the badge shows it.

  • Ammo: Content Credentials 3 pillars (capture, identity, content,

22 Jan 2026); C2PA hardware real (Leica/Sony/Nikon/Canon/S26) but

software verification is a desert (wave-8); our verifier layer is

the wedge.

  • Risk: don't claim C2PA compliance; say "C2PA-shaped", honest badge.
  • VERDICT: HIGH FIT, KIT-2 exists; label patch needed (provenance

nouns), 5 min.

MOCK 6: Accenture-flavored governed agent (Responsible AI DNA)

Statement: "An enterprise assistant that routes support cases across

email, tickets, and chat, and can take ACTIONS (refunds, access

grants, status changes) but only within a governance framework with

tiered approval, full audit, and a visible risk assessment per action."

Parse:

  • Company fingerprint: governance + risk + tiered approval + audit ->

Accenture (22%, CRAIO + Blueprint), Google (24% agentic).

  • Shape: 1 (governed agentic ops) + 5 (enterprise router).
  • Domain: enterprise ops / support triage.
  • Kit: KIT-5 (enterprise) or KIT-1; KIT-5 fixture is already loan-case

routing, noun swap to support cases.

  • Demo gate: one end-to-end case with the approval pause on stage,

then the refusal: agent attempts a destructive action, policy gate

blocks it, judge sees the risk tier + evidence panel (the 7-stage

approval queue from UI-UX-BRIEF).

  • Ammo: Accenture first Chief Responsible AI Officer; "Blueprint for

Responsible AI" framing; MAST/HCAST numbers; Replit/PocketOS

incidents as the why.

  • Risk: keep the demo to ONE workflow; SME white space line (wave-8).
  • VERDICT: HIGH FIT, KIT-5 verified 11/11, deck-agentic exists.
