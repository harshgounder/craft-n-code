COMPANY-LANE DEEP RESEARCH - Craft N Code 2026 sponsor question patterns

META

Reader: a 2nd-year E&CE student team competing in the Craft N Code 2026 Rajasthan state qualifier (MUJ, Aug 15-16). The problem statements drop at 21:30 IST Aug 15 and we pick our build lane within 10 minutes. The 5 official site tracks (Legacy/Night Ops/Signal/Open/Hardware) are a CLUB FALLBACK: insider confirmation says the real questions are set by the sponsor companies (Google, Apple, Meta/Facebook, Accenture, Adobe) and will be released at/just before the submission window. We need to predict, company by company, what problem families they set, so the decision tree fires on company DNA first. Context we already have (verified): 2022 D3 Fest problems (campus tools + web3 + ML), 2025 Rewind & Recode problems verbatim (NFT ticketing, web3 loyalty, P2P skill swap, AI lecture generator, Inbox Navigator, Digital TA lab grader, Mobile Packet Hunter), sponsor company hackathon DNA (Google Solution Challenge themes, Meta LlamaCon winners, Accenture Innovation Challenge themes, Adobe Creative Jam/Sparky AI, Apple Swift Student Challenge). We have a pre-built "one engine many skins" scaffold (ingest/summarize/rank/deadlines engine + complaint tracker + call-security product Kavach).

TASK (exhaustive, deep research)

Produce a comprehensive, evidence-cited intelligence report answering:

  1. GOOGLE lane: What problem statements has Google actually set or sponsored at Indian college hackathons (Google Solution Challenge, Google Developer Student Clubs hackathons, Google-sponsored fest hackathons like HackNITR/HackCBS/DevFest hackathons) in 2023-2026? List every concrete problem theme found with source URL. Which families recur (search/summarize, education, accessibility, AI-for-good)? What did WINNING teams build in Google-sponsored lanes? What is the probability Google sets an information-overload/search/summarization problem vs an education vs an accessibility problem?
  1. APPLE lane: Apple sponsors few hackathons directly; find every case where Apple/App Store/Swift was a hackathon sponsor or problem-setter in India 2023-2026 (Swift Student Challenge submissions, Apple Developer Academy hackathons, fest hackathons with Apple sponsorship). What did the problems look like? Mobile-first? Accessibility? Health/safety? What wins?
  1. META/FACEBOOK lane: Meta hackathons (Llama Impact, LlamaCon, Meta Build with AI) problem themes 2024-2026. Which Indian hackathons did Meta sponsor and what were the problems? Fraud detection, community/social platforms, content moderation, AI agents, multimodal? Winning project patterns (we know OrgLens/Compliance Wizards/Llama CCTV Operator). What is the likely shape of a Meta-set hackathon problem?
  1. ACCENTURE lane: Accenture Innovation Challenge + any Accenture-sponsored college hackathons 2023-2026. Exact problem themes (enterprise AI, patient care, manufacturing, business insights). What did winning teams deliver in each? How do Accenture problems differ from the others (enterprise framing, human-in-the-loop, template decks)? What is the likely shape of an Accenture-set problem at a 24h college hackathon?
  1. ADOBE lane: Adobe-sponsored hackathons in India (Adobe India hackathon, Creative Jam, dentsu x Adobe, Adobe x college fests) 2023-2026. Problem themes: creative AI, Firefly, media tools, no-code creative wrappers? Exact winning projects. What is the likely shape of an Adobe-set problem?
  1. CRAFT N CODE SPECIFIC: Any leak, past pattern, or organizer statement about how Craft N Code 2026 problems are selected: do sponsors write problems for the state rounds or only nationals? Has the Rajasthan round ever had sponsor-set problems before (2024 state round at MUJ, 2025 state rounds)? Search Unstop listings, CSC MUJ socials, past participant writeups, Reddit/Telegram/WhatsApp archives, organizer LinkedIn posts. Also: what are the 2026 sponsors' CURRENT product pushes (Gemini 3, Llama 4, Firefly, Swift, GenAI consulting) that would shape a problem written THIS month?
  1. PREDICTION MODEL: For each sponsor, output: (a) top 3 most likely problem families with probability estimates, (b) the exact problem text we'd expect (one paragraph, in their voice), (c) the winning-team skill profile, (d) what a team with a pre-built ingest/summarize/rank engine + call-security product should build, (e) the crowded-lane risk. Rank all 5 sponsors by total probability of being the one who sets OUR state-round question.

OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS

  • Markdown report, 30K+ characters, numbered sections matching the 7 tasks.
  • EVERY claim about a specific hackathon/problem/winner carries an inline citation URL [n] and every source URL appears in a Sources section at the end (deduplicated, 40+ sources minimum).
  • Data tables: sponsor x problem themes matrix; sponsor x winning project patterns; probability table with estimates and reasoning.
  • Verbatim problem statements where found, quoted exactly with source.
  • Explicitly flag what is VERIFIED (source seen) vs INFERRED (extrapolation) vs UNVERIFIED (rumor/hearsay).
  • End with the single most important paragraph: "If the question is set by X, Team 511 should build Y".
