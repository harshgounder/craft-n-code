WAVE: sponsor-product DNA + hottest tech + problem-to-solution mapping

Craft N Code 2026 state qualifier (Aug 15 21:30 drop) - Team 511

META

Reader: Team 511, 2nd-year E&CE students at MUJ competing in the Craft N Code 2026 Rajasthan state qualifier (overnight Aug 15-16, idea submission closes 06:00 Aug 16, pitch to judges 10:00-17:30 Aug 16). The REAL problem statements are written by sponsor companies (Google, Apple, Meta, Accenture, Adobe) - per club insider. We need: what each sponsor is CURRENTLY building/marketing (their flagship products, APIs, developer programs, hackathon themes for 2025-2026), what they are LOOKING for (their recruitment/devrel signals, what they reward in student hackathons), and the hottest tech they'd want student teams to use. Output must be concrete and evidence-cited: product names, API names, program names, dates, URLs. NO generic advice. This is prep for a 24-hour hackathon build.

Section 1: What each sponsor company is making RIGHT NOW (2025-2026)

For EACH of Google, Apple, Meta, Accenture, Adobe:

  • Their current flagship developer products and AI offerings (with exact names: models, APIs, SDKs, platforms)
  • Their 2025-2026 hackathon themes and challenge tracks (what problems they posed to student developers)
  • Their developer programs, grants, and incentives for students
  • What their job postings and DevRel content emphasize (what skills they're hiring for)
  • Verbatim problem-statement SHAPES they've used in student hackathons (2-3 examples each with source URLs)

Section 2: The hottest tech they'd reward in a 24h student build (2026)

  • AI agents and agentic workflows (frameworks, orchestration, tool-use patterns)
  • Multimodal AI (vision, audio, document understanding)
  • Generative media workflows (image, video, audio generation with provenance)
  • On-device / edge AI, mobile AI
  • Enterprise AI: RAG, evaluation, human-in-the-loop, audit trails
  • India-specific: Indic language support, UPI/financial AI, vernacular interfaces

For each: what it is, why a sponsor would reward it, what a 24h student MVP looks like, concrete example winners from 2025 hackathons.

Section 3: Problem-to-solution mapping (idea-lab method)

Using the evidence-mining approach (money flows, complaints, workaround intensity, second signals):

  • For each of the 6 sponsor companies: the 3 most likely problem shapes they'd set for a student hackathon, ranked by evidence
  • For each shape: the winning solution pattern (what the top 2025-2026 winners built), the 24h MVP scope, the demo that impresses judges
  • The 5 hottest cross-cutting themes that would win regardless of which sponsor sets the question

Output contract

  • Markdown report, 30,000+ characters
  • Every claim cited with a real URL inline
  • Data tables for sponsor comparisons
  • Label evidence: [VERIFIED] source seen, [INFERRED] reasoned prediction, [UNVERIFIED] rumor
  • End with a sources section listing every URL used
  • Concrete product/API/program names, never generic "AI is growing" statements
