2024 State Qualifier Format — RVCE (Bangalore) + 2024 National Problems

Compiled: 2026-08-13 | Source: cyb3r17/rvce-craft-n-code (GitHub), Mystery-Coder, Saiamarnath, pvjambur, adhithim09 repos

THE 2024 STATE QUALIFIER (VERIFIED — cyb3r17/rvce-craft-n-code)

  • Craft-N-Code 2024, RVCE (Bangalore) state round: 24h ONLINE hackathon, organized by RVCE Coding Club + IIIT Bhubaneswar
  • Theme: "AI and Big Data for food safety"
  • Dates: Oct 23-24 2024, 6:30 PM IST start
  • 3 tracks:
  1. Regulatory Compliance in Food Safety — automate compliance with AI/Big Data (scale problem)
  2. Food Safety Auditing Automation — continuous automated audits (manual = slow/error-prone)
  3. Sustainable and Safe Food Supply Chains — traceability, waste, contamination; optimize + secure the chain
  • Prizes: Top 2 teams → D³ fest national at IIIT-B, ALL EXPENSES COVERED trip. Others: participation certificates
  • Judging parameters (25% each):
  1. Project Completeness and Functionality — end-to-end product? scales in real world?
  2. Creativity in Design — innovations in design, comfortable UX
  3. Innovation in Idea — previously unsolved problem?
  4. Social Impact — impact on people's lives
  • Submission: GitHub Issues (Hackathon Submission template)

THE 2024 PATTERN (what the state rounds looked like)

  • ONE theme per state round (food safety for RVCE), 3 tracks within it
  • AI + Big Data + blockchain was the expected stack (4/5 solutions used blockchain!)
  • Hardware integration was a differentiator (Raspberry Pi + sensors)
  • Judging = 4 equal 25% buckets: completeness, design, innovation, impact
  • Top 2 advance to nationals with expenses covered

2024 NATIONAL FINALS PROBLEMS — STATUS

  • NOT recovered from public sources. The national finals (Nov 8, 24h at IIIT-B) problem statements were likely distributed on-site or via the D³ Fest portal (d3fest.tech 2024 snapshot not archived).
  • What we know: 1,318 registered / 280 players national; ₹30K/₹20K prizes; the state winners (incl. TrueMix from Bit-Binary-2027) competed.
  • The 2025 problems (7 challenges, recovered) + 2022 problems (9 challenges, recovered) give the pattern: the national finals re-skin the state themes into broader challenges.

INTEL IMPLICATIONS for 2026

  1. The 2024 state format = ONE theme + 3 tracks + 4×25% judging. The 2026 format = 5 tracks (PS-01..05) + sponsor-set questions. The judging buckets likely carry over: completeness, design, innovation, impact.
  2. Blockchain was the 2024 differentiator (4/5 solutions). The 2026 sponsors (Google/Apple/FB/Accenture/Adobe) suggest AI + security instead. But the "hardware hack" track (PS-05) echoes the Raspberry Pi solutions.
  3. The top-2-advance + expenses-covered model is the same as 2026 (top teams → IIIT-B nationals Oct 30-Nov 1).
  4. GitHub Issues submission in 2024 → the 2026 Supabase portal. The repo requirement is consistent across years.
