We need the anatomy of WINNING 24-hour hackathon builds so we can pre-build the reusable scaffold and mount any problem on it fast. Evidence-based: actual winners, actual repos, actual judging criteria.

Section 1: Winner anatomy - what actually wins 24h hackathons

  • 10-15 case studies of winning builds from 2024-2026 student hackathons (Smart India Hackathon, Google Solution Challenge, Meta LlamaCon, Adobe Creative Jam, Accenture hackathons, college fests like D3 Fest/Craft N Code)

  • Patterns: what the winners have in common (demo strategy, scope discipline, tech choices, zero-dependency rule, storytelling)

Section 2: The reusable scaffold for 24h builds

  • What components are reusable across 90% of AI-themed hackathon problems (ingestion, dedupe, ranking, summarization, deadline extraction, approval gates, audit logs, evidence panels)
  • The architecture that mounts any problem fastest
  • The demo-playbook: how winners demo in 3 minutes (structure, what to show first, how to handle failure)

Section 3: 2026 AI trends judges reward

  • The AI capabilities that won 2025-2026 hackathons and what's trending for late 2026
  • Agentic workflows, RAG with citations, multimodal input, human-in-the-loop safety, provenance
  • For each: what a 24h MVP looks like, what the demo shows, what judges ask

Output contract

  • Markdown, 25,000+ characters
  • Inline URLs for every case study
  • Tables for winner comparison
  • Evidence labels [VERIFIED]/[INFERRED]/[UNVERIFIED]
